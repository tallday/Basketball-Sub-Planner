B-Ball Subs

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

B-Ball Subs ("the App") is developed by Trent Allday. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you enter into the App (such as player names) is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server.

Analytics

The App may use anonymous analytics to understand general usage patterns, such as which screens are viewed. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users. No personal information is included in analytics data.

Data Storage

Player names and roster data are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not share any data with third parties. The App contains a link to an optional feedback form hosted by Google Forms. If you choose to submit feedback, that submission is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is suitable for use by all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via the feedback form within the App.
