Frequently Asked Questions

How many players can I add?

You can add up to 10 players. A minimum of 5 players is required to generate substitutions.

What game format does the app use?

The app assumes a 40-minute game with two 20-minute halves and 5 players on court at a time.

How is playtime calculated?

The algorithm distributes court time as evenly as possible across all active players. Each player gets roughly the same total minutes.

Are my players saved?

Yes. Your roster is saved locally on your device and will be there when you reopen the app. No account or sign-up is needed.

Does the app collect my data?

No personal data is collected. All player data stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I clear my roster and start fresh?

Remove players one at a time using the remove button next to each name, or clear the app's data from your device settings.

The app isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, please let us know via the feedback form with a description of the problem.
